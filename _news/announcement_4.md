I’m pleased to share that I’ve completed the *Delivering Quality Work with Agility* course, offered by IBM through Coursera. This program provided a deeper, more structured understanding of agile practices and how to consistently deliver high-quality work in dynamic environments.

Throughout the course, I strengthened several key skills, including:

- **Agile mindset and frameworks**: applying iterative, value-driven approaches to projects.  
- **Quality-first delivery**: ensuring clarity, precision, and measurable outcomes in every stage of work.  
- **Continuous improvement**: refining processes through feedback, retrospectives, and workflow optimization.  
- **Effective collaboration**: aligning teams around shared goals and improving communication loops.  

These insights have further enhanced the way I plan, execute, and refine my work, and I’m excited to integrate these principles into future projects.
