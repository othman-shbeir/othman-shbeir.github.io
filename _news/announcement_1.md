I’ve started serving as a **Teaching Assistant** on **July 29, 2025**. This role lets me support students directly while sharpening my own foundations in software engineering and teaching practice.

---

#### What I’m assisting with

<ul>
  <li>Leading weekly labs and walkthroughs</li>
  <li>Holding office hours for debugging, architecture, and API design</li>
  <li>Reviewing assignments and giving actionable feedback</li>
  <li>Preparing short primers on JavaFX, Spring, JDBC, JPA,Java Streams, and REST best practices</li>
</ul>

Beyond logistics, I’m focused on helping students reason about trade-offs (performance vs. readability, DX vs. security) and build confidence shipping real features.

---

#### Highlights & tools

- Spun up example repos for MVC structure, queue jobs, and HTTP clients.
- Demoed request validation, rate limiting, and idempotency patterns.
- Shared a mini-guide on SQL query shaping and eager loading.
- Showed how to translate Figma specs into Vue components with the Composition API.

> “We learn best by building—and by explaining what we’ve built.”

---

#### What’s next

I’ll publish short **how-to notes** for common pitfalls (auth flows, pagination, and transactional boundaries) and run optional study groups before exams. If you’re in my section and want a topic covered, ping me and I’ll prioritize it.
